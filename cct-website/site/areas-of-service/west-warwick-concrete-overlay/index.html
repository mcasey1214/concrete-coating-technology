A new layer over
existing concrete.

A concrete overlay adds a finishing layer over an existing surface. Options described for this service include changing texture, color, or pattern and resurfacing tile or linoleum.

Possible applications include sidewalks, walkways, driveways, patios, commercial floors, and exterior pavement.

Preparation depends on the surface and the work planned. Contact us to discuss your project.
