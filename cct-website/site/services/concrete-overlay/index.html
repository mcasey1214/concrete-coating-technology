Concrete Overlay

A fresh surface. A whole new possibility.

A concrete overlay adds a new finishing layer over suitable existing concrete. Before choosing a look, we need to understand the surface beneath it.

WHAT THIS SERVICE DOES

A new layer over existing concrete.

Concrete Overlay is the process-focused service for updating an existing concrete surface. It covers the questions of preparation, application, and curing before a finish is selected.

An overlay may change the look or texture of a residential or commercial surface, but not every slab is suitable for a new layer. For wood-, stone-, brick-, or slate-inspired looks, explore our Decorative Concrete Overlays.

WHEN TO EXPLORE IT

When is an overlay worth discussing?

  • An existing home or business surface you want to update.
  • Concrete that may support a new finishing layer after its condition is reviewed.
  • A project where the finish, texture, and preparation need to be considered together.
YOUR OPTIONS

Prepare. Apply. Cure.

We think about an overlay in three stages. The details depend on the material and the concrete in front of us.

01

Prepare the surface

Cleaning, removing old coatings, repairing imperfections, or diamond grinding may be needed before a new layer. The existing concrete determines the preparation.

02

Apply the selected material

The chosen overlay or coating is applied after preparation. Options we can discuss include epoxy and polyaspartic, with choices in color, texture, and decorative finish.

03

Allow it to cure

Curing is the period when applied material sets and hardens. Timing depends on the material and project, so we discuss the schedule for your surface.

WHAT TO EXPECT

First, see what the slab can support.

An overlay will not fit every concrete surface. Its condition, any old coating, the space’s use, and the finish you want all affect the next step. We can discuss options for homes and businesses without assuming a method or timeline in advance.

WANT TO LEARN MORE?

More about the overlay process.

Open a topic for more detail, or ask Melissa about your specific concrete.

1. Prepare the concrete+

The surface is cleaned and old coatings may be removed. Preparation can also include repairing imperfections and diamond grinding. Diamond grinding uses a machine with rotating abrasive segments to prepare the concrete for another layer.

2. Apply the selected material+

After preparation, the selected overlay or coating is applied. We can discuss epoxy and polyaspartic options, with choices in color, texture, or decorative finish.

3. Allow the surface to cure+

Curing is the time an applied material needs to set and harden. Timing depends on the material and project, so we discuss the schedule for your surface before work begins.

Explore decorative applications+

Decorative looks include patterns inspired by wood, stone, brick, or slate. See our decorative concrete overlays for more finish choices.
