Patterns and texture+

A stamped or textured cement-based overlay can create wood-, stone-, brick-, or slate-inspired patterns. Listed wood tones include walnut, oak, driftwood, and gray barnwood. Other pattern choices include flagstone, Ashlar slate, herringbone brick, random stone, and European fan.

Color and coating options+

Epoxy flake and quartz finishes use a different approach from stamping an overlay. CCT also lists acid stains, water-based colors, and metallic epoxy for indoor floors among its decorative options. The existing concrete and selected method affect the appearance.

Preparation and application+

A stamped or textured overlay may involve repairing and diamond-grinding existing concrete, adding a cement-based layer, then pattern, color, and sealer. Stains and decorative coatings follow different steps. For surface questions, explore Concrete Repair & Maintenance.

Where a decorative finish may fit+

CCT lists residential and commercial spaces including driveways, patios, garages, basements, pool decks, walkways, lunchrooms, salons, and car dealerships. The concrete condition, indoor or outdoor setting, and how the space is used help determine which finish to discuss.
