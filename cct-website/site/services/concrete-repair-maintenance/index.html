WHAT THIS SERVICE DOES

Start with the slab you have.

Concrete Repair & Maintenance starts with understanding the condition of your existing concrete. Grinding, repair, sealing, or resurfacing may be worth discussing, depending on the surface and how you use it.

A worn floor does not automatically call for a new slab. Some surfaces can be prepared for a different finish; others need a different plan after we look at them.

WHEN TO EXPLORE IT

When should we take a look?

  • A garage floor or other concrete showing wear, cracks, or surface imperfections.
  • A surface that may need attention before a coating or overlay.
  • A project where you want to understand alternatives to full replacement.
YOUR OPTIONS

Ways to work with what is there.

The right scope depends on the slab. We can talk through practical choices without assuming one finish fits every surface or budget.

01

Repair and preparation

Grinding and addressing surface imperfections can prepare existing concrete for the next step. The extent of repair depends on what we find.

02

Sealing and maintenance

Sealing is one option described for existing concrete. We can discuss whether it makes sense for the surface and your goals.

03

Resurfacing and overlays

A new finishing layer may be an option when the concrete can support it. See how our Concrete Overlay service approaches preparation, application, and curing.

Our repair and maintenance services include grinding, repairing, and sealing existing concrete. The work to consider depends on the condition and use of the surface.

Surface preparation+

Preparation gets existing concrete ready for the next step. It can include grinding and addressing surface imperfections before a coating or overlay is applied.

When an overlay may fit+

An overlay adds a new finishing layer over existing concrete. If you are considering a different texture, pattern, or color, explore our concrete overlay and decorative overlay services.
