WHAT THIS SERVICE DOES

A coating chosen for the space.

Protective coating systems add a layer over prepared concrete. An appropriate system can help protect a surface from everyday wear while giving it a different color, tone, or finish.

We discuss epoxy, polyurea, and polyaspartic options for residential and commercial concrete. The surface condition, setting, and use matter more than a product name alone.

YOUR OPTIONS

Epoxy, polyurea, or polyaspartic?

These are coating types, not interchangeable promises. Melissa can help you compare them in the context of your surface and project.

01

Epoxy

Epoxy resin is mixed with a hardener to form a coating layer. Color, tone, and finish effects can vary. Ask which epoxy option, if any, suits your floor and its use.

02

Polyurea

Polyurea forms when two ingredients mix and react. Formulas differ, so the specific product and the condition of your concrete both matter.

03

Polyaspartic

Polyaspartic is a type of polyurea coating used on concrete floors. Ask how the available option fits an indoor or outdoor setting and the way the space is used.

WANT TO LEARN MORE?

Coating terms in plain language.

Open a topic for more detail, or ask Melissa about your specific concrete.

Epoxy+

Epoxy floor coating is made by combining epoxy resin with a hardener. The mixture forms a layer on the concrete. Color, tone, and finish effects can vary.

Polyurea+

Polyurea forms when two ingredients mix and react to make the coating. One is an isocyanate; the other is an amine-based resin. Formulas vary by product.

Polyaspartic+

Polyaspartic is a type of polyurea coating used on concrete floors. Ask us which coating options suit the surface and project.

Places to consider a coating+

We can discuss coatings for commercial and industrial floors, patios, walkways, pool decks, warehouses, and healthcare settings. For an outdoor project, ask which coating or decorative finish, if any, suits the surface.
