# CCT typefaces

The logo's stencil lettering informs two restrained accent typefaces. The homepage uses **Black Ops One** for short industrial labels. Other pages retain **Saira Stencil One** for short labels and service indices. Editorial serif headlines and readable sans-serif body copy remain in place.

## Black Ops One

- Family: Black Ops One, regular 400; self-hosted Latin WOFF2 subset at `cct-website/site/fonts/BlackOpsOne-Latin.woff2` with `font-display: swap`.
- Original TTF and SIL Open Font License 1.1 are preserved beside it as `BlackOpsOne-Regular.ttf` and `BlackOpsOne-OFL.txt`.
- Source: [Google Fonts family metadata](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/blackopsone/METADATA.pb) and [official Google Fonts specimen](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Black+Ops+One).

## Saira Stencil One

- Family: Saira Stencil One, regular 400, self-hosted as the Latin WOFF2 subset in the website; the original TTF and OFL license are also retained in `cct-website/site/fonts/`.
- Designers: Héctor Gatti and Omnibus-Type
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1
- Delivery: self-hosted Latin WOFF2 subset under `cct-website/site/fonts/`, declared with `font-display: swap`; the TTF source file is also retained beside it.
- Font project and license: [Google Fonts family record](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/sairastencilone) and [upstream Saira Stencil project](https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Saira/tree/master/SairaStencilOne)

The font's OFL license text is bundled next to the font as `cct-website/site/fonts/OFL.txt`. The accent font is not used for paragraphs or long headings. System sans-serif fallbacks remain in the CSS.
